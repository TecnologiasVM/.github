A small, founder-led studio working together since 2018. One throughline has run through everything: we integrate emerging technologies, several at once, to solve problems the conventional stack can't reach. We started deep in data — Elasticsearch, search, social analytics — and that work sharpened into intelligence: from statistical reports to AI-validated insight, from search to RAG, GraphRAG and multi-agent systems. Now we ship AI-powered products end to end, for our clients and for ourselves.

## What we do

| | |
|---|---|
| **Data & Search** | Making sense of complex information at scale — extracting, indexing, searching across millions of documents. |
| **Analytics & Intelligence** | Turning raw signals into insight: social behavior, collective thinking, the patterns behind real decisions. |
| **AI & Multi-Agent Systems** | Agentic systems that reason over knowledge graphs and surface answers backed by source documents, not guesses. |
| **AI Product Development** | Full AI products end to end, from the data layer to the interface. |
| **Technology Validation** | Mapping where a technology really sits before betting on it — including the approaches that don't survive. |
